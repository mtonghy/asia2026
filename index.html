How to ride

T-money

Buy a T-money card at any convenience store (₩3,000–5,000) and top it up with cash. Tap in and tap out at the gates — the base fare is about ₩1,550. Trains run roughly 05:30–24:00.

Use Naver Map or KakaoMap rather than Google for live Korean transit directions — Google's transit data in Korea is limited. Both have English modes.

Seoul · Kakao T cars + metro
Chuseok falls during your stay: the public holiday runs Thu Sep 24 – Sat Sep 26, with Chuseok day itself on Fri Sep 25. Subway, buses, taxis and convenience stores run as normal and the palaces and hanok villages stay open (palaces are usually free those days), but many independent restaurants close — especially on Friday. Phone or check Naver Map before going to Daol (Thu) and Odarijip (Fri); hotel restaurants, department-store food halls and the big Myeongdong/Hongdae chains are the safe fallback. A typical day

the daily rhythm · one outing, one rest
How every day is shaped: late start, one main outing in the morning, a sit-down lunch, a real rest back at base mid-afternoon, then a short local wander and dinner close to home. Arrival, departure and day-trip days break the pattern — those are spelled out in each city's Day-by-day section. Times are a guide, not a schedule.

Slow start at the flat

Capsule coffee and a simple breakfast in the kitchen, or a bakery run on the Hongdae street. Nobody leaves before 9:30 — mornings on the subway are crowded until then.

10:00
Head out — one main sight

Walk ~7 min to Hongik Univ Station 홍대입구역 for Line 2, or call a Kakao T from the door. One anchor per day: a palace, Bukchon, N Seoul Tower or Myeongdong.

Morning sightseeing

About two hours on foot, with benches and a tea-house stop built in. Palaces and hanok lanes are best before the midday tour groups.

Lunch near the sight

Sit-down Korean lunch close to wherever you are — kalguksu, ssambap, bibimbap. Lunch is the big meal; queues ease after 13:15.

14:30
Back to Hongdae, feet up

Subway or Kakao T home. A proper 60–90 minute rest at the flat — this is what keeps five days sustainable.

16:30
Easy local wander

Hongdae shopping street, Yeonnam-dong cafés or the Gyeongui Line Forest Park — all flat, all within a 10-minute walk, no transit needed.

18:30
Dinner

Korean BBQ or a neighbourhood spot near the flat, or a Kakao T to one of the saved restaurants. Buskers come out on the main street after dark.

20:30
Home by half eight

GS25 for tomorrow's water and snacks, laundry on if needed, early night.
