Airport transfers — exact details

Bus 6002Stop #8₩17,000

Arriving — get off at "Dongmyo Station" 동묘역 (stop #8 on the 6002 route). Coming from the airport the order is … Jongno 1-ga → Jongno 6-ga → Heunginjimun (Dongdaemun) → Dongmyo ← get off here → Sinseol-dong → Cheongnyangni. So Dongmyo is the stop right after Dongdaemun/Heunginjimun — when you see the big old gate, you're next.

On arrival the bus drops you on the south side of Jong-ro by Dongmyo Station — your flat is then ~50 m from the station itself, essentially across the road. (The airport-bound stop for your return is a different one, ~250 m east on the north side — details in the departure box below.)

Boarding at the airport: Terminal 1 → stand 6A-1 (1F, in front of gates 5–6). Terminal 2 → stand 30 (Transportation Centre, B1). Buy the ticket at the counter or self-service machine before boarding — you can't just tap T-money outbound. Fare ₩17,000; two bags each included. Ride is ~90–110 min via Hongdae and Jongno.

How to ride

T-money

Buy a T-money card at any convenience store (~₩4,000) and top it up with cash. Tap in and tap out at the gates — a typical hop is ₩1,400–1,600. Trains run roughly 05:30–24:00.

Use Naver Map or KakaoMap rather than Google for live Korean transit directions — Google's transit data in Korea is limited. Both have English modes.

Arrival timing: AC 63 leaves YVR midday Sun Sep 20 and lands ICN 16:05 on Mon Sep 21 (crosses the date line). Check-in is from 15:00, so the timing works — with the ~90-min bus you'll reach Dongmyo around 18:00.

★ 4.4Neon · canal

The neon heart of Osaka and the Glico sign — a few minutes' walk from your hotel. Best after dark; eat on the side streets, not the canal front.

Osaka Castle & Park

★ 4.4History

Iconic keep set in a huge moated park. Book tickets online to skip the line; the grounds alone are worth the walk. 9–6.

Umeda Sky Building

★ 4.4Sunset deck

Floating Garden Observatory with a 360° open-air deck — go for sunset into night. Glass-tunnel escalators up are part of the fun.

Tsutenkaku & Shinsekai

★ 4.1Retro · kushikatsu

Old-school Osaka tower with a slide and walk decks, ringed by neon kushikatsu joints. Great for an atmospheric evening.

Tempozan Ferris Wheel

★ 4.5Bay views

Big wheel over Osaka Bay (~¥1000, 15 min), next to the aquarium. Sunset or night for the best ride; clear-floor cabins available.

Day trips: Kyoto / Nara

~30–45 min

Seven nights is plenty to add Kyoto's temples or Nara's deer park — both short train hops from Namba. USJ is also nearby if that's your thing.

Day-by-day

Osaka · transit trains
Getting around Osaka: trains are the easy way here — frequent, punctual, and elevators at all major stations. Grab an ICOCA card (tap on/off). Each stop shows 🚆 Train or 🚶 Walk, the estimated time, and a directions button with live platforms. A taxi is always an option for tired moments.
OCT10SAT
Late arrival · sleep at the airport hotel

Land KIX at 21:15. After immigration and bags, walk straight from arrivals to the Hotel Nikko Kansai Airport at the terminal — no train tonight. Drop your bags and rest. A grab-and-go konbini dinner is all you'll need.

🚶 WalkKIX arrivals → Hotel Nikko Kansai Airport~5 min

Osaka Castle & park

Train to Osaka Castle. The park is broad and flat with shuttle options; enjoy the grounds and the keep's exterior without the climb, or take the lift inside. A calm, green half-day. Easy lunch, then back to rest.

🚆 TrainNamba → Osaka Castle~20 min
Flat park · lift inside
OCT13TUE
Day trip: Nara (deer & gentle paths)

Easy train to Nara. The deer park is famously flat and just minutes from the station — feed the bowing deer, see Todai-ji's great hall, sit under the trees. Go at your own pace and return mid-afternoon. One of the calmest, most charming days of the trip.

🚆 TrainNamba → Nara (Kintetsu)~45 min
🚶 WalkNara Station → Nara Park & Todai-ji~10 min
Flat · near stationHalf/full day, your call

Slow day in Namba

Recover after the day trip. Late start, in-room laundry, a leisurely okonomiyaki lunch at Kuuya, and an easy potter around the local arcades. Sunset at the Umeda Sky open-air deck if you're up for one short outing.

🚆 TrainNamba → Umeda Sky Building (optional)~15 min
Easy dayOkonomiyaki KuuyaOptional Umeda sunset

Day trip: Kyoto (the gentle highlights)

Smooth train to Kyoto. Keep it relaxed — pick one or two flat, atmospheric spots: the Gion streets with a riverside tea, or Fushimi Inari's famous torii gates (you can see the iconic stretch near the entrance without the full hill climb). Long lunch, unhurried return.
