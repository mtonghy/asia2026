Leaving early on Sat Sep 26 → ICN

CX 413 departs ICN Terminal 1 at 08:00 — aim to be at the airport by 05:30–06:00. This is the Saturday of the Chuseok holiday, so expect a busier-than-usual terminal.

  1. Simplest: pre-book a taxi for ~04:30 (Kakao T reservation, or ask the host to arrange one). About 50–60 min at that hour; expect roughly ₩60,000–100,000 for the car.
  2. Cheaper: airport bus 6002. The first airport-bound buses leave central Seoul just after 04:10 and run every 12–15 min, so they pass Hongdae from roughly 04:30–05:00. ~60–70 min to T1. ₩17,000 each, tap T-money or pay cash (no change given).

How to ride

T-money

Buy a T-money card at any convenience store (₩3,000–5,000) and top it up with cash. Tap in and tap out at the gates — the base fare is about ₩1,550. Trains run roughly 05:30–24:00.

Use Naver Map or KakaoMap rather than Google for live Korean transit directions — Google's transit data in Korea is limited. Both have English modes.

Arrival timing: AC 63 leaves YVR midday Sun Sep 20 and lands ICN 16:05 on Mon Sep 21 (crosses the date line). Check-in is from 16:00, so the timing works — after immigration and bags you will be on the road around 17:00 and at the flat roughly 18:00–18:45.
